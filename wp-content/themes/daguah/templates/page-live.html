Market Live

How it works

Shop a market stall from your sofa

  1. 1

    A stall goes live from the market.

    The seller walks you through the pieces in daylight, so you see the true colour and the weight of the cloth.

  2. 2

    Ask as you watch.

    Questions go to the seller in the stream's chat. Every piece shown is in the tray below the player.

  3. 3

    Buy from the tray.

    Your payment is held by DAGUAH Hold until the parcel reaches you, as with every order.
