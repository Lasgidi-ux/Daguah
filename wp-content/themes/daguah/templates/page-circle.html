DAGUAH Circle

How a circle works

One cloth, the whole family, every country

  1. 1

    Choose the cloth and send the link.

    The host picks the fabric, how many yards each person needs and when the circle closes.

  2. 2

    Everyone joins and pays their own share.

    Each person pays in their own currency, from wherever they live. The board shows who has joined and who has paid.

  3. 3

    The cloth comes from one bolt.

    So the colour matches on the day. It goes to each person's door, or all of it to the host to share out.
